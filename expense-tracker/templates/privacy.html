Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for Spendly, we collect basic account information such as your name and email address. When you use the service, we also collect the expense data you choose to enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We do not collect:

  • Payment card or bank account details
  • Government-issued identification
  • Location data beyond what your browser voluntarily provides
  • Data from third-party financial institutions

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to operate Spendly and provide you with a personalised expense tracking experience.

Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns
  • Allow you to filter and search your own records
  • Send account-related communications such as password resets

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the service to you.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely and access is restricted to your account only. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We use industry-standard practices to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss.

While we take reasonable precautions, no system is completely immune to security risks. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your Spendly account.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information or financial data with any third parties under any circumstances, except where required by law.

If we are legally compelled to disclose information (for example, in response to a court order), we will notify you to the extent permitted by law before doing so.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. At any time, you may:

  • Access and review all expense records stored under your account
  • Edit or delete individual expense entries
  • Request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data for personal use

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the app. Account deletion requests will be processed within 7 days, after which all your data will be permanently and irreversibly removed.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Spendly after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have any questions or concerns about how we handle your data, please reach out to us directly through the app.
